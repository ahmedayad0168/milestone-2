# Shipping Policy

**Policy version:** 1.1 (demo policy)

## Processing and delivery

Orders are normally prepared within 1–2 business days after payment confirmation. Delivery estimates depend on destination and carrier availability. The demo knowledge base contains no live carrier tracking integration.

## Delays

If a shipment is delayed beyond the displayed estimate, customer support should verify the order status and carrier information before communicating a revised date. Do not invent a delivery date.

## Address changes

Address changes may be requested before an order is handed to the carrier. After dispatch, support must contact the carrier; the assistant cannot edit shipping addresses.
