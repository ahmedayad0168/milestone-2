# Customer Data Handling Rules

**Policy version:** 1.0 (demo policy)

Use customer information only to complete a specific support or operational request. Retrieve a customer record only when the requester provides the relevant email address. Do not list all customers, infer personal details, or expose unnecessary fields.

The demo data is synthetic. Real deployments must authenticate staff, enforce role-based access, log access to personal data, minimize returned fields, and follow the organization's applicable privacy and retention requirements.
