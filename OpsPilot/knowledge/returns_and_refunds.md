# Returns and Refunds Policy

**Policy version:** 1.2 (demo policy)

## Eligibility

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days after delivery. Items must be unused, unopened, and in their original packaging. Proof of purchase is required. Perishable, custom-made, and hygiene-sensitive products are not eligible unless they arrived damaged or incorrect.

## Opened or used items

An opened or used item is normally not eligible for a change-of-mind return. If it is defective, damaged in transit, or the wrong item was delivered, customer support should collect photographs and the order number and escalate the case for a review.

## Refund timing

After an eligible return is received and inspected, the refund is approved by a support supervisor. The payment provider may take 5–10 business days to show the refund. The assistant must not promise a refund or initiate one; it may only explain the policy and recommend escalation.

## Escalation

Cases outside the standard eligibility rules must be reviewed by a support supervisor. Do not infer eligibility from incomplete information.
