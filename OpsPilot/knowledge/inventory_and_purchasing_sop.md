# Inventory and Purchasing SOP

**Policy version:** 1.0 (demo policy)

## Reorder trigger

A product should be reviewed for replenishment when its available stock is less than or equal to its configured reorder level. The reorder level is a planning threshold, not an automatic purchase instruction.

## Purchase approval

A department manager must review and approve every replenishment request before a purchase order is sent to a supplier. A draft request should include product ID, product name, current stock, reorder level, requested quantity, supplier, business reason, and request date.

## Supplier lead time

The standard planning assumption for the demo business is 7 calendar days. High-priority shortages should be flagged to the manager; the assistant must not claim a delivery date without a confirmed supplier response.

## Inventory updates

Only an authorized inventory operator may update on-hand stock after receiving and checking the goods. An AI-generated recommendation or draft must never directly change inventory quantities.
